Admission Process & Online Enquiry (2026–27)

Transparent, merit-and-interaction based admission process for Nursery to Class IX and Class XI (Science, Commerce & Humanities).

4-Step Admission Procedure

  1. 1

    Submit Registration / Enquiry

    Fill out the online admission enquiry form on this page or collect the prospectus from the school reception between 8:30 AM and 2:00 PM.

  2. 2

    Campus Visit & Interaction / Baseline Test

    Informal child-parent interaction for Nursery–Class II. Written baseline competency test in English, Math & Science for Classes III to IX & XI.

  3. 3

    Document Verification

    Submit Birth Certificate, Previous Class Report Card, Transfer Certificate (TC), Passport Photos (4), and Aadhaar/APAAR ID copy.

  4. 4

    Fee Deposition & Seat Confirmation

    Confirm admission within 7 working days of selection by depositing the Term-1 fee via UPI, NetBanking, or Demand Draft at the fee counter.

Scholarship Provision (Classes IX & XI)

Up to 50% Tuition Fee Waiver for students scoring above 92% in Class VIII / Class X Board Exams or National Sports/Olympiad Medalists.
