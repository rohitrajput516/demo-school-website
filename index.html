Principal’s Message

“Education is not merely the filling of a vessel, but the kindling of a lifelong flame of curiosity, courage, and character.”

Dear Parents and Students, warm greetings from Takshashila Vidya Mandir. In an era defined by rapid technological transformation, our responsibility as educators extends far beyond preparing students for board examinations. We prepare them for life.

Every child enters our campus with a unique spark. Whether a student excels in Mathematics and Robotics, shines on the athletics track, or expresses creativity through literature and debate, our faculty ensures personalized mentorship so no child goes unnoticed.

We strongly believe that meaningful education is a three-way partnership between the student, the teacher, and the parent. I invite you to visit our campus, interact with our faculty, and join us in shaping a bright, disciplined future for your ward.

Dr. Meenakshi Rathore Principal, Takshashila Vidya Mandir
