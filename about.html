About Our Institution

Nurturing Curiosity, Character, and Competence Since 1998

Founded in 1998 under the aegis of the Takshashila Educational & Welfare Society, Takshashila Vidya Mandir was established with a clear purpose: to bring metropolitan-standard academic infrastructure and value-based education within reach of every aspiring family.

Following the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and CBSE curriculum framework, our pedagogy moves beyond rote memorization. We emphasize conceptual clarity, scientific temper, communication skills, and physical fitness.

Our Vision

To cultivate self-reliant, compassionate, and globally competent citizens who lead with integrity and scientific inquiry.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, inclusive, and intellectually stimulating environment supported by dedicated mentors and modern technology.

Core Values

Satyam (Truth), Shram (Diligence), Sanskar (Respect), and Samarpan (Dedication to Community & Nation).

Principal’s Message

“Education is not merely the filling of a vessel, but the kindling of a lifelong flame of curiosity, courage, and character.”

Dear Parents and Students, warm greetings from Takshashila Vidya Mandir. In an era defined by rapid technological transformation, our responsibility as educators extends far beyond preparing students for board examinations. We prepare them for life.

Every child enters our campus with a unique spark. Whether a student excels in Mathematics and Robotics, shines on the athletics track, or expresses creativity through literature and debate, our faculty ensures personalized mentorship so no child goes unnoticed.

We strongly believe that meaningful education is a three-way partnership between the student, the teacher, and the parent. I invite you to visit our campus, interact with our faculty, and join us in shaping a bright, disciplined future for your ward.

Dr. Meenakshi Rathore Principal, Takshashila Vidya Mandir
