World-Class Facilities & Co-Curricular Life

Designed for safety, scientific exploration, and holistic physical development across an 8.5-acre Wi-Fi enabled campus.

01

Smart Digital Classrooms

Spacious, ventilated classrooms equipped with 75-inch interactive flat panels, 3D visual learning modules, and ergonomic seating.

02

Composite Science & STEM Labs

Separate, fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Atal Tinkering Robotics labs accommodating 40 students simultaneously.

03

Computer & Coding Centre

Dual high-speed computer laboratories with 1:1 student-to-system ratio, gigabit fiber internet, Python IDEs, and firewall security.

04

Knowledge Resource Library

Over 18,000 books, NCERT reference banks, competitive magazines, periodicals, and a quiet digital e-library reading zone.

05

Sports Complex & NCC

400m athletics track, basketball & volleyball courts, cricket turf nets, indoor badminton/chess hall, and active NCC Junior Division troop.

06

GPS-Tracked Fleet & CCTV Security

24x7 CCTV surveillance across campus, trained female attendants, on-campus infirmary, and GPS-enabled school buses covering 30+ routes.
