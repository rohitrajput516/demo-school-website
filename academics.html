Curriculum & Wings

Academic Programs (5+3+3+4 NEP Structure)

Structured learning pathways designed to build strong fundamentals in early years and competitive excellence in senior classes.

Ages 3 to 8 Years

Foundational Stage (Nursery to Class II)

Activity-based, joyful Montessori and play-way learning focusing on phonics, numeracy, motor skills, storytelling, and social etiquette.

  • Bilingual Phonics & Early Literacy
  • Number Sense & Puzzle Lab
  • Art, Craft, Music & Nature Walks
  • No-Homework Policy up to UKG
Classes III to V

Preparatory Stage (Primary Wing)

Transitioning to structured discovery through experiential classroom learning, mental mathematics, environmental awareness, and computer basics.

  • English, Hindi & Sanskrit Foundation
  • Hands-on Science & Math Kits
  • Introduction to Coding & Digital Safety
  • Public Speaking & Reading Club
Classes VI to VIII

Middle Stage (Junior Wing)

Deepening subject expertise across Sciences, Mathematics, Social Studies, and AI/Coding with Olympiad preparation and laboratory exposure.

  • Integrated Physics, Chemistry & Biology
  • Atal Tinkering Lab (Robotics & IoT)
  • National Olympiad & NTSE Foundation
  • Mandatory Vocational Skill Modules
Classes IX & X

Secondary Stage (Board Foundation)

Rigorous CBSE board preparation with weekly competency-based tests, practical lab sessions, career counseling, and doubt-clearing clinics.

  • Standard & Basic Mathematics Options
  • Information Technology (Code 402) / AI
  • Fortnightly CBSE Pattern Mock Tests
  • One-on-One Academic Mentorship
Classes XI & XII • Science

Senior Secondary: Science Stream (PCM / PCB)

Synchronized CBSE Board + competitive foundation (JEE / NEET / NDA) with advanced Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, and Computer Science labs.

  • Physics, Chemistry, Math / Biology
  • Computer Science (Python & SQL) / PE
  • Integrated JEE, NEET & Defence Guidance
  • Dedicated Practical & Numerical Clinics
Classes XI & XII • Commerce & Arts

Senior Secondary: Commerce & Humanities

Comprehensive preparation for future Chartered Accountants, Civil Servants, Economists, and Legal Professionals with CUET & CLAT orientation.

  • Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics
  • History, Political Science, Geography
  • Applied Mathematics / Informatics Practices
  • CUET, CA Foundation & NDA SSB Orientation
